# Research Query Reference

Search query patterns used by the three parallel research subagents.

## Company Profile Queries

```
"{company} company overview products services"
"{company} funding revenue valuation 2025"
"{company} business model customers go-to-market"
"{company} CEO leadership team"
"{company} market share position"
"{company} latest news 2025"
```

## Competitor Research Queries

```
"{company} competitors alternatives 2025"
"top companies in [industry]"
"{company} vs [competitor]"
"[competitor] review strengths weaknesses"
"[industry] market leaders"
```

## Market Analysis Queries

```
"[industry] market size TAM 2024 2025 forecast"
"[industry] CAGR growth rate"
"[industry] trends opportunities 2025"
"[industry] challenges risks regulation"
"[industry] Porter five forces analysis"
"[industry] barriers to entry"
```

## Tips

- Search in both Chinese and English for Chinese companies (e.g., "字节跳动" AND "ByteDance").
- Use year qualifiers ("2024", "2025", "latest") to prioritise recent data.
- Fetch full pages from annual reports, analyst reports, and Crunchbase for financial data.
- Cross-reference market size from at least 3 independent sources.
