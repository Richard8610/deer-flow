# Competitive Analysis Prompts

Prompt templates used by the competitive analysis workflow.
These are the base prompts; the agent fills in {company} at runtime.

## Company Research Prompt

Research the company "{company}" thoroughly:
- Business overview, products/services, business model
- Team, funding/revenue, market position
- Recent news (past 12 months)

## Competitor Research Prompt

Identify and profile the top 3–5 competitors of "{company}":
- Name, overview, strengths vs target, weaknesses vs target
- Market share, pricing comparison
- End with a comparison table

## Market Research Prompt

Analyze the market that "{company}" operates in:
- Market size (TAM), CAGR, current stage
- Top trends 2024–2025, growth drivers
- Barriers to entry, key risks
